# ZineSpore

An underground zine and gig platform on Nostr, built with [nostr-post](https://github.com/saintego/nostr-post).

Gig calendars, band and album wikis, posters, reviews and concert reports, styled like a photocopied zine. Venues can embed their own calendar on their own site.

**Local scenes, global reach.** You post in your local spore: your city, your venue, your crew. What you post isn't locked there. A gig created in Prague shows up for a fan in Berlin, and an album review written in Czech can be read in German or English, through translations by people or a machine draft. Each scene keeps its own look, editors and filters; the information is shared by all of them.

ZineSpore is the working name. The final brand will be decided after a domain and handle check.

See [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) for the full concept and plan.
